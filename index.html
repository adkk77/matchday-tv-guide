Data source

API-Football gives live scores and the match minute for 1,000+ football leagues. Get a free key (100 requests a day) at dashboard.api-football.com and paste it here.

TV channels come from TheSportsDB and ESPN. A TheSportsDB premium key adds more channel listings (optional).

My channels. One rule per line: competition = channel. Every match in that competition gets the channel, shown with a dashed border, when the data feeds list none. The first matching rule wins.

Use on your phone. Copy this link and open it on your phone (send it to yourself in WhatsApp or Notes). It copies your keys and channel rules there. It contains your API key, so keep it to yourself. Then use Add to Home Screen from that page: the icon remembers your settings.
